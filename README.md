# botq-2026
Repository of work from 'Battle of the Quants' Fall 2026 at Brigham Young University

The objective of the competition was to construct a portfolio and development a multi-asset signal strategy whose performance
improves on the benchmark of the Russell 3000.

Our strategy was to invest in stocks that graded above a certain level using a composite score taking into account
momentum, beta, and Idiosyncratic volatility.
